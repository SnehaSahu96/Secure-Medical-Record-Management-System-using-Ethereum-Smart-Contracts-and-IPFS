Your records, encrypted and in your control

Files are encrypted in your browser, stored on IPFS, and referenced by a smart contract on Sepolia. The contract decides who may get the decryption key.

How it works: encrypt file, upload ciphertext to IPFS, save the CID on-chain; a doctor requests the key, the server checks the contract, and the file is decrypted locally.

Stack: HTML/CSS/JS, ethers.js, MetaMask, Solidity, IPFS (Pinata), Node backend. Use fictional data only. Blockchain data is public; revoking access cannot erase files already downloaded.
